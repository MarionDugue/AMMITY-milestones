### Rethinking Waste as a Resource

This presentation was the first, to our knowledge, to explore the use of menstrual blood in a space context. 

<blockquote style="font-size: 1.2rem; font-style: italic; color: var(--accent); border-left: 3px solid var(--accent); padding-left: 1rem; margin: 2rem 0;">
    "What happens if we stop considering this biological byproduct as traditional 'waste', and instead view it as a valuable resource for deep space missions?"
</blockquote>

### Presentation Highlights

Our primary objective was to open up the discussion of menstruation in space for both practitioners and non-experts alike. We achieved this by:

*   **Breaking the Taboo:** Initiating an open, scientific dialogue about menstrual health in aerospace.
*   **Innovative Case Study:** Exploring the potential of using menstrual blood-derived stem cells for therapy—specifically in the context of counteracting microgravity-induced bone density loss.
