### Objectives:
The first parabolic flight testing the first (rather rudimentary) menstrual cup prototype adapted to microgravity. We had 4 parabolas of around 6s of reduced gravity to test the collection mechanism of menstrual blood. 

### Menstrual cup design:
V1: suction system appended to the menstrual cup stem. Collection action by sucking the menstrual blood using an attached syringue. 

### Achievements:
- First time testing a menstrual cup in a reduced gravity context! 
- V1 testing 

### Lessons learnt:
 - 3 vomit bags that were initially thought as unnecessary were soon very much needed.
