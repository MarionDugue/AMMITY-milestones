### Rethinking Waste as a Resource (Part 3)

We are back at MELISSA! This conference is a main reason to how the AMMITY project had been started and is continuing today. This time, we presented our prototypes (v1-v3) and lessons learnt from our 2 parabolic flight camapaigns. We opened up discussion on collaborating for the next step once the collection device is finalised: research on using menstrual blood in a spaceflight context.
