What if the key to deep space crewed mission lies in a biological waste we currently discard?

The AMMITY project (Advancements for Menstrual-blood Management in reduced gravITY) was started in 2021 to integrate menstrual health into the space exploration roadmap. Our current research is now focusing on developing a menstrual collection device that can collect the fluid in a reduced gravity context. It can then be repurposed to fit in the closed life support systems aboard spacecrafts.

Explore below the timeline of the project and the work we have thus far achieved.
